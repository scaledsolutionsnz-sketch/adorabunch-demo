About Adorabunch

Flowers with Heart and Soul

Adorabunch is Ashburton's local florist, bringing beauty and warmth to the mid-Canterbury community. Behind every arrangement is Donna — a passionate florist who believes flowers have the power to say what words sometimes can't.

Whether it's a romantic bouquet, a heartfelt sympathy tribute, or wedding flowers that take your breath away, Donna puts genuine care and creativity into every single stem she touches.

Local, personal, and made with love. That's the Adorabunch difference.
